# 16 — Sources and Credits

## Purpose
This document defines how research and media sources are cited in the website and how credit information is maintained.

## Research citation format
For each source record, store:
- source ID;
- author, maker, performer or institution;
- title;
- publication or recording date;
- source type;
- stable URL;
- access date;
- relevant page, section, object number or timestamp;
- claims supported;
- reliability notes;
- quotation/permission status where relevant.

## Website presentation
Use concise inline source links near the relevant content, with a fuller Sources and Credits page. Do not hide essential provenance only in a global footer. For interviews, credit according to consent and the agreed attribution format.

## Media attribution
Follow the exact licence terms. Include creator, title, source, licence and required link where applicable. Do not imply that a source's general website terms grant permission to reuse every asset.

## External discovery portals
These are starting points for research discovery, not citations for any specific instrument claim. Researchers must locate and cite the exact record or publication used.

- Sangeet Natak Akademi: https://sangeetnatak.gov.in/
- Indira Gandhi National Centre for the Arts: https://ignca.gov.in/
- Centre for Cultural Resources and Training: https://ccrtindia.gov.in/
- Smithsonian Folkways: https://folkways.si.edu/
- British Library Sound and Moving Image catalogue: https://www.bl.uk/collection-guides/sound

Portal availability, catalogue contents and reuse conditions must be checked at the time of research.
