# 03 — Tradition Selection

## Approved scope
The project scope discussed in the approved concept is **6–8 Indian musical traditions explored deeply**.

## Missing selection record
The exact names of the selected traditions and the rationale for selecting each were not present in the accessible files used to build this package. This document intentionally does not invent or substitute a list.

## Required selection register
Before content population, restore or approve a register with:
- stable tradition ID;
- preferred name and alternate names;
- language/script and transliteration conventions;
- geographic associations, with source evidence;
- musical contexts and instrument families;
- communities and practitioner perspectives represented;
- rationale for inclusion;
- research depth and source coverage;
- known exclusions, contested boundaries and gaps.

## Selection criteria
Use a balanced, transparent set of criteria:
1. relevance to the project's human-centred instrument/craft narrative;
2. availability of credible documentation and practitioner voices;
3. diversity of instrument construction and sound-producing mechanisms;
4. diversity of materials and craft processes;
5. geographic and cultural breadth without tokenism;
6. feasibility of ethically sourced media;
7. ability to explore each selected tradition with depth.

## Do not
- select traditions solely because images are easy to find;
- equate a state or region with one homogeneous musical tradition;
- infer an instrument's exclusive origin from a single modern location;
- use a tradition name as a substitute for documenting communities and internal diversity.
