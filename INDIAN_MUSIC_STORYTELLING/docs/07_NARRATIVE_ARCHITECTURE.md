# 07 — Narrative Architecture

## Experience model
A guided human journey and an independent archive share the same content records. They are not separate products.

## Proposed guided arc
1. **Before sound:** introduce the relationship between people, materials and place.
2. **Made by place:** explore geographical context and material availability.
3. **Meet the instrument:** introduce forms and musical roles.
4. **Look inside:** explore anatomy and sound-producing components.
5. **The making:** follow documented craft processes.
6. **The people:** meet makers and musicians.
7. **Hear it:** listen to sourced recordings and understand performance context.
8. **Across traditions:** compare carefully selected relationships.
9. **The living archive:** conclude with continuity, adaptation and knowledge transmission.

This is an experience architecture, not a claim that all traditions follow the same historical or craft sequence.

## Independent entry points
- Geography / place
- Tradition
- Instrument
- Material
- Craft process
- Maker
- Musician
- Sound / recording

## Content page pattern
Each detail page should include:
- title and short orientation;
- key image with credit;
- concise overview;
- optional deeper sections;
- connected entities;
- source references;
- clear return and onward paths.

## Narrative voice
Curious, precise, respectful and accessible. Explain unfamiliar terms. Attribute interpretations. Avoid romanticising or presenting living practices as frozen heritage.
