# 01 — Project Brief

## Working title
**Beyond the Sound** (working title; retain only if confirmed as the final project title).

## Concept
An interactive, editorial living archive that reveals Indian musical instruments as the result of relationships between place, materials, craft knowledge, makers, musicians, performance and cultural life.

The website should not behave like a static encyclopedia or a grid of unrelated instrument cards. It should guide visitors through a human story while allowing them to enter the archive independently through an instrument, region, material, maker, process or sound.

## Proposition
Discover not only what an instrument is, but how it is made, who makes and plays it, what knowledge it carries, and how it participates in a musical culture.

## Audience
A broad public audience, including curious first-time visitors, students, design and music enthusiasts, and visitors seeking deeper instrument or craft knowledge. Content must be understandable without specialist knowledge while offering optional depth.

## Objectives
1. Make selected Indian musical traditions approachable without flattening their differences.
2. Explain instrument anatomy and sound production through clear visual interactions.
3. foreground documented makers, musicians and knowledge transmission.
4. Connect materials, construction techniques, geography and cultural context.
5. Make sound an optional, user-controlled part of discovery.
6. Provide traceable sources and respectful representation.

## Scope
Approximately 6–8 traditions, selected and researched deeply. The exact approved list is not available in the accessible source materials and must be restored from the approved research record before content production.

## Out of scope for the initial release
- User accounts and personalised profiles.
- Public user-generated content.
- Unverified crowdsourced claims.
- A complex live database or unnecessary external APIs.
- Automated AI-generated biographies or cultural explanations.
- Unlicensed audio/video hosting.

## Success criteria
A visitor can find an instrument, understand its components, follow its making process, encounter a documented maker or musician, listen to an approved recording if available, and trace key claims to sources. The experience remains usable with audio off, motion reduced, JavaScript-heavy visualisations unavailable, or media missing.
