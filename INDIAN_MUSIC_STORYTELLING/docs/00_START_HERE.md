# 00 — Start Here

## Purpose
This pack is the handoff specification for an interactive Indian musical-instrument storytelling website. It consolidates the approved concept, visual direction, UX architecture and technical recommendations into a traceable development brief.

## What is approved
- A human journey through Indian musical instruments, their makers, craftsmanship and cultural significance.
- A content scope of approximately 6–8 traditions, explored deeply rather than superficially.
- Two complementary discovery modes: guided storytelling and independent exploration.
- Visual direction: **The Living Archive, in Motion** — editorial, accessible, immersive, with meaningful sound and motion.
- Editorial typography using Fraunces or Instrument Serif, paired with a highly legible sans-serif such as Inter.
- Progressive disclosure: introduce essential context first and reveal deeper technical/cultural detail when requested.
- Interactions should communicate real research, not merely decorate the interface.

## What is not available in this handoff
The exact approved tradition list, verified instrument inventory, maker and musician biographies, craft-process evidence, source-level citations, original Indian research spreadsheets and licensed media files were not available in the accessible files. These are recorded as gaps, not silently reconstructed.

## Build order
1. Read the project brief and research methodology.
2. Resolve the tradition selection against the approved research source.
3. Populate datasets from verified sources; preserve originals.
4. Review narrative and visual direction.
5. Implement shared layout and design system.
6. Implement data-driven exploration components.
7. Add media only after rights and attribution checks.
8. Test responsive behaviour, accessibility, provenance and fallbacks.

## Non-negotiable rules
- Never fabricate facts, citations, people, dates, regional associations or media permissions.
- Keep source records separate from normalized website data.
- Every factual content record must have one or more source IDs, or an explicit status explaining why it is not verified.
- Unknown is not equivalent to false, absent or not applicable.
- No autoplaying audio or video.
- Every interaction must have a meaningful outcome and a non-visual/accessibility alternative.
