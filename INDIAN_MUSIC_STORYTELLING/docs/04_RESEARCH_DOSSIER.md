# 04 — Research Dossier

## Research question
How do selected Indian musical instruments connect place, material, making knowledge, makers, musicians, sound and cultural meaning?

## Narrative research lenses
### Place
Document geographic associations, movement, exchange and the limits of mapping. Use multiple locations where evidence indicates circulation or distributed practice.

### Instrument form and function
Record instrument classification using a named classification system where appropriate. Distinguish physical components, sound-producing mechanism, playing technique and performance role.

### Materials
Record material names at the level supported by evidence. Distinguish species or material identification from broad labels such as wood, metal, fibre or gourd. Include local terminology where verified.

### Craftsmanship
Document stages, tools, techniques, workshop variation, specialist roles, repair and maintenance. Do not present one maker's method as universal.

### People and knowledge
Document maker and musician identities, roles, lineage or training only where sourced and appropriate. Record consent and attribution requirements.

### Sound and musical context
Describe sound qualities with careful language and, where possible, link to recordings and performer explanations. Avoid unsupported pseudo-technical claims.

### Cultural significance
Use attributed explanations and contextual evidence. Avoid assigning one fixed symbolic meaning to an entire community or instrument.

## Per-tradition dossier template
For each approved tradition, prepare:
1. overview and terminology;
2. communities and geographic context;
3. historical evidence and change over time;
4. instruments in scope;
5. materials and construction;
6. makers, workshops and transmission;
7. musicians, performance and repertoire context;
8. sound and playing techniques;
9. contemporary practice, adaptation and preservation;
10. contested claims and research gaps;
11. source list and media rights notes.

## Current evidence status
No tradition-specific research record was available in the accessible source files. This dossier therefore defines the research structure; it does not claim that the research has been completed.
