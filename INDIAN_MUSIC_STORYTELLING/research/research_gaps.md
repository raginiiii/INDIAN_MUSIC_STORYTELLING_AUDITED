# Research Gaps and Limitations

## Critical gaps
1. **Approved tradition names:** the approved scope is 6–8 traditions, but the exact names were not present in the accessible source files.
2. **Original Indian research datasets:** no Indian instrument spreadsheet, source notes, interview transcripts or prior research database were available.
3. **Instrument records:** no verified inventory, classifications, anatomy, playing techniques or source-linked descriptions were available.
4. **Maker and musician records:** no verified profiles, interview permissions or attribution preferences were available.
5. **Materials and craft processes:** no instrument-specific evidence was available to substantiate materials, tools, techniques or process order.
6. **Geography:** no verified region associations, boundary definitions or map data were available.
7. **Media:** no Indian project image, video or audio assets with verified provenance and permissions were available.
8. **Claim-level bibliography:** no source register supporting Indian instrument claims was available.

## Source file inspected
The accessible uploaded PDF is titled `Chatgpt Create Research Plan.pdf`. Its contents concern a Beatles discography and lyrics data-storytelling project, including Beatles songs and playback research. It is unrelated to the Indian musical-instrument project and has not been used as evidence or copied into the Indian project datasets.

## Consequence
The package is development-ready as an architecture and documentation scaffold, but it is not a completed factual research corpus. It must not be described as containing verified tradition/instrument research.

## Required next inputs
- the approved list of 6–8 traditions;
- original research notes and references;
- any existing instrument/maker/material spreadsheets;
- source photographs, audio and video references;
- any prior approved research dossier or bibliography.

## Safe implementation while gaps remain
Develop the visual system, navigation, reusable components, empty states, schemas and test fixtures that are clearly labelled as non-factual. Do not publish invented cultural content to make the prototype look populated.
